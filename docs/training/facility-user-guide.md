# RUTF-DTAP — Guide for facility users

**Who this is for:** the person who records RUTF stock at a State store, an LGA store or an OTP
site. **Time to learn:** about one hour, with a phone in hand.

**What the app is for:** the app keeps a record of every sachet of RUTF that comes in, goes out
and is given to children at your facility, so that stock can be planned and gaps noticed early.
Checks the system makes are **for review, not accusation** — a difference usually means a
recording slip, a delayed delivery or a counting error, and your State reviewer looks at it with you.

---

## 1. Before you start

- Sign in with the email and password you were given. You only see **your own facility**.
- Connect to the internet once after signing in so the app can download your facility, the RUTF
  lots and any deliveries on their way to you. After that, **everything works offline**.
- Your Home screen shows only the actions for your facility:

| Action | State / LGA store | OTP site |
| --- | --- | --- |
| My Stock | ✓ | ✓ |
| Receive Stock | ✓ | ✓ |
| Issue Stock | ✓ | — |
| Daily Dispensing | — | ✓ |
| Stock Count | ✓ | ✓ |
| Record Loss / Adjustment | ✓ | ✓ |

**Never record child or caregiver names, phone numbers or ID numbers anywhere in the app** — not in
notes, not in photos. OTP figures are daily totals only.

---

## 2. My Stock

Shows how many sachets you hold of each lot, which lots expire soon ("Use first"), which have
expired, deliveries on their way to you and any open Findings at your facility.

- The figures are **as of the last sync**, plus what you have recorded on this phone since.
- Tap **Refresh from server** when you have a connection.

## 3. Receive Stock (4 steps)

Use this the day a delivery arrives.

1. **What are you receiving?** Pick the transfer on its way to you (the lots and quantities are
   filled in from what the sender issued), or **Supplier delivery** for stock not issued through
   this platform (for example a direct UNICEF delivery).
2. **Lots received** — check each lot against what arrived. Change the quantity if it differs.
   Enter cartons and loose sachets; the app works out the total. Record any damaged on arrival.
3. **Evidence** — waybill / delivery note number, and a photo of the waybill (optional, but it
   settles questions later). Make sure the number, date and signatures are legible.
4. **Review** and submit.

If you received less than was issued, enter what you actually received — the difference is
recorded and the State reviewer follows it up with the sender.

## 4. Issue Stock (stores only, 4 steps)

1. Choose the facility you are issuing to (only facilities you supply are listed).
2. Lots and quantities — the app warns if you issue more of a lot than it thinks you hold, and
   suggests the lot that expires first.
3. Issue voucher number and photo (optional).
4. Review and submit. The transfer is created when it syncs; the receiving facility sees it as
   "on its way" and confirms it with Receive Stock.

## 5. Daily Dispensing (OTP sites, one screen)

At the end of each OTP day, copy the **day's totals** from the OTP register:

- New admissions
- Children seen (including new admissions)
- Sachets prescribed
- Sachets dispensed

Only one entry per day. A photo of the register page is optional — if you take one, **cover every
child and caregiver name first** and tick the box confirming it.

> Record every OTP day, even when it was quiet. A week with no dispensing recorded shows as a
> reporting gap.

## 6. Stock Count (3 steps)

Count what is **physically on the shelf**, lot by lot. The month-end count is due **by day 5 of the
next month**; you can count on any other day too.

1. Count date.
2. Count every lot (enter 0 if none), and how many of each are expired.
3. Only now does the app show the ledger figure next to your count — this is deliberate, so the
   count isn't influenced by what you expect. Review and submit.

If the count differs from the ledger by more than the limit (5%, or 10% for hard-to-reach sites),
a Finding goes to the State reviewer. Recount before submitting if a figure looks wrong.

## 7. Record Loss / Adjustment (3 steps)

1. **What happened** — Damaged, Expired, Other loss, or an Adjustment (a correction up or down);
   the lot and the quantity.
2. **Reason and evidence** — explain in a few words; a photo of the damaged stock or the stock
   card entry helps.
3. Review and submit.

Adjustments are confirmed by your supervisor on their next visit. Expired stock should be recorded
as an expired loss once it is set aside.

---

## 8. Syncing — what to do when something is waiting

- Every screen says **"Saved — it will sync when you're online."** Your record is safe on the phone.
- The banner on Home shows how many records are still waiting. Open **Sync Queue** to see them and
  tap **Sync Now** when you have a connection.
- A record that fails five times is marked **Failed**. Tap **Retry**. If it fails again, call your
  LGA focal person — the platform administrators see the same error on their side
  (Devices & Sync) and can tell you what to fix.
- **Do not uninstall the app or clear its data while records are waiting** — they would be lost.
- Sync at least once a day when you can, and always before month-end.

## 9. Quick checks

| Situation | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| A delivery arrived but isn't in the list | Refresh; if it still isn't there, record it as a **Supplier delivery** and note the waybill number. |
| You entered the wrong figure and already submitted | Don't enter a second copy. Small differences: record an **Adjustment** with the reason ("typed 1000 instead of 100 on 3 Sept"). A whole entry that is wrong (entered twice, wrong day, wrong facility): ask your Project Admin to **void** it — it stays visible, struck through, and you can then record it correctly. |
| The lot number on the carton isn't in the list | Choose **A lot not in the list…** and type it exactly as printed, with the expiry date. |
| The app says you hold less than you are issuing | Count the shelf. If the shelf is right, issue it and record a Stock Count the same day. |
| You see a Finding at your facility | It is a question for review, not a judgement. Your reviewer will contact you; have the stock card and waybills ready. |
