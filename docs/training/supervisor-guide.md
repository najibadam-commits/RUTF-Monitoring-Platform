# RUTF-DTAP — Guide for supervisors

**Who this is for:** LGA Nutrition Officers who supervise OTP sites, and State officers who
supervise the LGA stores and the State store. **Time to learn:** about two hours, including one
practice visit.

Your visits check that what is recorded in the platform matches what is on the shelf and in the
register. Findings your visit raises go to the State reviewer **for review, not accusation**.

---

## 1. Your scope

- Your account says which level you supervise — **stores** (LGA stores + the State store) or
  **OTP sites** — and in which LGAs. You can only record visits there.
- Home shows: **Store Check** (store supervisors) or **OTP Verification** (OTP supervisors),
  and **Confirm Adjustments**.
- Sync before you set out: the app downloads the facilities you cover, their ledger balances and
  recent dispensing. Visits then work fully offline.

## 2. Store Check (7 steps)

| Step | What you do |
| --- | --- |
| 1. Store | Choose the store you are visiting. |
| 2. GPS | Capture your location **at the store**. More than 150 m from the store's recorded point is flagged on the visit (not a Finding) — note why if the point is wrong. |
| 3. Stock card | Is there a stock card for RUTF? Is it up to date (every receipt and issue entered)? Enter the stock card balance. |
| 4. Count by lot | Count every lot on the shelf (0 if none), with expired and damaged sachets. **The ledger figure is not shown yet** — count first. Use *Add a lot found on the shelf* for a lot that isn't listed. |
| 5. Storage | Dry? Off the floor? Ventilated? Signs of pests? First-expiry-first-out followed? |
| 6. Evidence | Photos of the stock card and the stack (optional), and a **selfie at the facility** (required). |
| 7. Review | Your count against the ledger, and the **Findings this visit will raise**. Check anything surprising before you submit. |

## 3. OTP Verification (7 steps)

| Step | What you do |
| --- | --- |
| 1. OTP and period | Choose the OTP site and the period to recount (usually the last full month). |
| 2. GPS | As for a store check. |
| 3. Register | Is the OTP register available? A missing register is recorded as a Finding. |
| 4. Recount | From the register, count **new admissions** and **sachets dispensed** for the period. **Totals only — no names.** |
| 5. Count by lot | Count the OTP's RUTF on the shelf. |
| 6. Evidence | Register page photo **with every child and caregiver name covered** (tick to confirm), stock photo, and the selfie. |
| 7. Review | The verification factor (your register count ÷ what the app recorded) and the Findings the visit will raise. |

The verification factor should be between **0.90 and 1.10**. Outside that range a
`dispensing_gap` Finding is raised for review — often it means days were missed in the app, or
the register and the app were filled from different sheets.

## 4. Confirm Adjustments

Facility users record corrections ("adjustments") between visits. On your visit, check each one
against the stock card and the shelf, then tap **Confirm adjustment** with a note. Unconfirmed
adjustments above 2% of the balance raise a Finding.

## 5. What raises a Finding from your visit

| Finding | When |
| --- | --- |
| Stock variance | Your count differs from the ledger by more than 5% (10% hard-to-reach) |
| Expired stock | Expired sachets still on the shelf |
| No stock card | No RUTF stock card at the store |
| Poor storage | Not dry, stock on the floor, or pests seen |
| Register missing | OTP register not available |
| Dispensing gap | Verification factor outside 0.90–1.10 |

## 6. Rules you must follow

- **Never photograph a child or caregiver.** Register photos only with names covered.
- **Never write child or caregiver names, phone numbers or IDs** in notes.
- Record the visit **at the facility**, on the day — the GPS and selfie are what make your visit
  evidence.
- Your Risk-Based Planner (on the web, Risk-Based Supervision) lists the facilities due a visit this month, highest risk
  first. Aim for your monthly workload (8 visits).

## 7. Syncing

Same as for facility users: every visit is saved on the phone ("Visit saved — it will sync when
you're online") and goes up with photos when you have a connection. A visit that keeps failing is
marked **Failed** in Sync Queue — tap **Retry**, and if it still fails, contact the platform
administrator, who sees the same error on Offline Sync Health. Don't clear the app's data while
anything is waiting.

## 8. Practice visit checklist (training day)

- [ ] Signed in, synced, the facilities you cover are listed
- [ ] Store Check or OTP Verification completed at the training venue, every step
- [ ] Register photo taken with names covered (use the training register)
- [ ] Visit synced; it appears on the web under Supervision → Risk-Based Supervision
- [ ] One adjustment confirmed with a note
