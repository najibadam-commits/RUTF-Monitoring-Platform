| R2 | 2026-10-02 | Proposal runs on generated dummy data (`data/dummy/`), every row tagged `DUMMY`. | Najib: real DRIs, OTP list, stores, DHIS2 not at hand; replace during implementation. |

| R14 | 2026-10-02 | Data Sets shows **Facilities, Lots, Dosing rules, Thresholds**; Settlements / Activity Sites are no longer shown (tables kept). Dosing rules and thresholds are programme sign-off values (§12.3–12.4). |
| R15 | 2026-10-02 | A facility user (`dri`) is assigned **one facility** in Users & Roles, chosen from **stores and OTP sites only** (307 today). | R16 | 2026-10-02 | Facility visibility (RLS): a `dri` sees only its facility, its supplier and the facilities it supplies; other roles see approved facilities in their project. | R17 | 2026-10-02 | `derive_facility_location()` fills a blank ward from coordinates and **flags, never blocks**, a point outside its declared LGA/ward (`location_flag`; 12 registry rows flagged, filterable in Data Sets).
